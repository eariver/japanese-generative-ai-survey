## Source-local observations

- The Deployment Safety Hub distinguishes the August versions released on August 6 from the July versions that remain in use in Codex and ChatGPT Work.
- It describes the August release as replacing GPT-5.5 Instant for the relevant ChatGPT experiences, with a new default model for Free and Go users and an updated GPT-5.6 Sol with an effort slider for Plus and Pro users.
- Under the Preparedness Framework, the page says the August GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna release is treated as High capability in Cybersecurity and Biological and Chemical domains, while neither reaches the High threshold in AI Self-Improvement.
- The page states that safety evaluations use the lowest reasoning deployment settings to represent most usage, while capability assessments use maximum reasoning effort to estimate an upper bound.
