## Source-local observations

- OpenAI describes an August 6 ChatGPT update: GPT-5.6 Sol is updated for Plus and Pro users with more focused answers and improved factual reliability, and a new slider lets users choose how much thought is used for a response.
- OpenAI says the same model now powers Instant and deeper reasoning experiences for Plus and Pro users.
- OpenAI says GPT-5.6 Luna becomes the default model for Free and Go users, with unlimited text chats and a Think button for questions needing more reasoning, subject to the stated rollout/guardrails.
- The page describes the update as a ChatGPT product change, not the original GPT-5.6 model launch.

## Attribution and limitations

- The reliability, access, rollout, and product-behavior descriptions are OpenAI's claims; the page's internal evaluation figures are not independently reproduced here.
- OpenAI explicitly says this optimized Sol version is available only in the Chat experience and that the GPT-5.6 Sol version powering ChatGPT Work and Codex is not changed by this release.
- This capture therefore represents a distinct August ChatGPT/product-behavior update and does not rewrite it as the July model launch.
