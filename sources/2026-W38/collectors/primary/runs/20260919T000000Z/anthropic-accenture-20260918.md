# Partnering with Accenture on embedded evaluation (excerpt)

We're partnering with Accenture on independent evaluation of frontier AI — a step toward the commitment in CEO essay "We Must Pace the Frontier" to embed evaluators within Anthropic.

Led by Faculty (Accenture's specialist AI business): evaluating/red-teaming models, alignment assessments, safeguards testing. Accenture brings enterprise deployment perspective.

Each of Anthropic and Accenture expects to invest at least $1 billion in building capacity in this area over the next five years.

Embedded evaluators work inside AI companies with employee-comparable access: watch models take shape in training, follow build/deploy decisions, speak directly to employees; assess operations, verify safety commitments, identify blind spots; report incidents; give public informed account of benefits/risks.

Anthropic funds Accenture's work directly for now. Dialogue with METR and other nonprofit evaluators to pilot embedded evaluation with their own funding.
