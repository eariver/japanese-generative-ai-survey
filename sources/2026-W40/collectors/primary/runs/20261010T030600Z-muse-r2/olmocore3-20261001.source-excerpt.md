> "October 1, 2026"
> "Today we're releasing [Olmo-core 3](https://github.com/allenai/olmo-core), a significant upgrade to our framework for developing large language models featuring a redesigned open mixture-of-experts (MoE) training system."
> "Our earlier MoE implementation in Olmo-core used fully sharded data parallelism (FSDP) ... Olmo-core 3 switches to a system based on [distributed data parallelism (DDP)](https://narrative.allen.ai/scaling-up-training#data-parallelism). It keeps experts resident on GPUs and routes the relevant data to them"
> "In a preliminary test on eight NVIDIA B300 GPUs, a 47-billion-parameter MoE processed 52,000 tokens per second per GPU with the new stack, compared with 19,400 using our earlier implementation—about 2.7x the throughput."
> "In one benchmark, we increased the expert pool from 8 to 128 while still selecting only four experts per token ... Total parameter capacity grew from 4.6B to 47B, while training throughput fell by less than 5%."
> "We've benchmarked Olmo-core 3 across a range of configurations on NVIDIA B300 GPUs, including a 1.2-trillion-parameter model with 58.36 billion parameters active per token across 512 GPUs. Its highest observed throughput was 858 TFLOP/s/GPU"
> "reaching a configuration with 2.38 trillion total parameters. This was a short-capacity test rather than a full training run"
