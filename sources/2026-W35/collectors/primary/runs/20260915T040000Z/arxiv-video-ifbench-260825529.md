arXiv:2608.25529 [cs.CV] — Video-IFBench: Evaluating Instruction Following of Multimodal LLMs in Video Understanding Scenarios. Authors: Hongbo Liu, Peixian Chen, Sihan Liu, Peiyuan Zhang, Kai Zou, Dian Zheng, Xiaoxing Hu, Yuhao Dong, Mengdan Zhang, Yunhang Shen, Haoyu Cao, Wei Liu, Weibo Gu, Xing Sun, Shengjie Zhao. Submitted 26 Aug 2026. DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2608.25529.

Abstract: MLLMs show strong video-understanding performance, but instruction-following in this domain is under-explored; real-world use requires satisfying diverse user-specified constraints, incl. visual/audio-grounded ones. Existing benchmarks focus on task accuracy over instruction adherence. Video-IFBench: instruction taxonomy with 4 templates (single-task, multi-task, selection, nested), 32 task types, 39 manually designed constraint categories (semantic + format). Semi-automatic construction (MLLMs + programmatic processing + human verification): 1.5K samples. Evaluation of 20+ recent MLLMs: video instruction following remains challenging, esp. many-constraint, semantic-constraint, and complex conditional (branch/path selection on video content) instructions.
