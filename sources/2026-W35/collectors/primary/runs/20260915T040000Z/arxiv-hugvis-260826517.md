arXiv:2608.26517 [cs.CV] — HUG-VIS: A Multimodal Benchmark for Human-centered Understanding and Generation in Visual Intelligence. Authors: Fei Ma, Zebang Cheng, Minghui Li, Hongbo Xu, Yuyong Tan, Yihua Shao, Hanling Wang, Zhou Liu, Yuqing Gao, Dong Wang, Long Ma, Laizhong Cui, Nicu Sebe, Qi Tian. Submitted 27 Aug 2026. DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2608.26517.

Abstract: human-centered visual intelligence couples vision+audio+language across 4 tasks: human emotion recognition, human video generation, human voice cloning, human video matting. Existing resources are task-specific; HUG-VIS is a unified benchmark: 8,400 seated half-body videos of 30 professional actors, same 280 emotion-action-prompt assignments each, controlled Mandarin studio protocol, synchronized video/audio/text/alpha mattes. Zero-shot eval of diverse open/closed models (auto metrics + criterion-specific MOS + cross-task analyses). Findings: (i) linguistic content dominates emotion recognition; purely visual affect weakest; (ii) video generation + voice cloning: auto metrics and human judgment agree overall but differ on top rankings — joint reporting required; (iii) boundary fidelity under motion is main remaining matting obstacle; (iv) difficulty varies across emotions/models/metrics with cross-task correlations.
