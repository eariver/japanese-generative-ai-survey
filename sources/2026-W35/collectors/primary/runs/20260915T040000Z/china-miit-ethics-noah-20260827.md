Title: China advances AI regulation with upstream ethics review and over 200 standards. By Aiden Captain Culture, Aug 27 2026.

MIIT confirmed nearly 200 AI standards formalised and a city-level ethics review pilot operating across multiple locations, per ministry remarks at a State Council Information Office briefing in Beijing on 26 August 2026 — from policy outline to active compliance framework.

Centrepiece: organisations doing AI R&D must put projects through ethics review BEFORE development begins. Trial guideline issued in April by MIIT + nine departments: human well-being, fairness, controllability; scrutiny of training-data choices, algorithm design, bias/discrimination steps; enterprise/university/research ethics committees; risk-management tools; better datasets.

Contrast: EU AI Act = pre-market checks before deployment; China pushes review upstream into research phase. City pilot launched May in provinces with national AI industrial innovation zones (review centres, training materials, national risk-monitoring network); now broadened to active city-level operation (review/service centres, ethics-risk database, "Ethics Classroom" training). Covers data/algorithms/models + manufacturing, education, healthcare, finance, consumer services. Overlap waiver to avoid duplicate paperwork.
