Molmo2-Cap

Molmo2-Cap is a dataset of very long (average 900 words), detailed video captions. It can be used to pre-train and fine-tune vision-language models.

Molmo2-Cap is part of the Molmo2 dataset collection and was used to train the Molmo2 family of models.

Quick links:

  • 📃 Paper
  • 🎥 Blog with Videos

Data Format

Videos are stored as Youtube video ID that will need to be downloaded separately.

Clip Annotations

clip_timestamps: a list of video clips' start and end timestamps.

clip_transcripts: a list of audio transcripts of annotator describing the video clips.

clip_captions: a list of clean captions converted from the above audio transcripts using LLM.

Frame Annotations

frame_timestamps/frame_indices: a list of timestamps/indices of sampled frames.

frame_captions: a list of frame captions generate by Molmo.

Video Annotations

video_transcript: audio transcript of annotator describing the video.

video_caption: clean caption converted from the above audio transcript using LLM.

merged_caption: long caption combining both clip_captions and video_caption using LLM.

video_frame_merged_caption: long caption combining both clip_captions, video_caption, and frame_caption using LLM.

We suggest to use either merged_caption or video_frame_merged_caption.

Other

annotation_score: the score (0-10) of the quality of the caption judged by LLM. High score indicates a good amount of visual details and low score indicates the inclusion of off-topic meta commentary or content irrelevant to the videos.
