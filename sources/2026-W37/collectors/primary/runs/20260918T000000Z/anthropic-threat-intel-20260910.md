# Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

Over the past eight months, our Threat Intelligence team identified and disrupted operations in which threat actors tried to use Claude for malicious activity.

This report covers activity we disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026 across seven harm areas: cyber operations, influence operations, surveillance, scams and fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons development, and distillation. Claude Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus models were used. None of the misuse cases involved the use of Claude Fable or Mythos-class models, with the exception of one illicit distillation case.

Cases include suspected state-sponsored groups, financially motivated criminals, commercial spyware vendors, state propaganda institutions, politically motivated individuals.

Trends: Sophisticated attacks no longer require sophisticated attackers; AI role increasingly autonomous via multi-agent frameworks; humans set targets and review exfiltration.

Case GTG-20006: Russian espionage (Midnight Blizzard consistent), 20+ orgs, Ukraine/Europe focus, AI-automated phishing, malware rebuild on detection, WhatsApp takeover, camera streams, 300k identity records exfiltration (vendor-reported).

Case GTG-50014: ShinyHunters smash-and-grab, credential harvesting, 1.8M APKs, SaaS supply-chain theft, vibe hacking pattern.

Case GTG-10007: Exploit foundries, Chinese-speaking operators, autonomous vulnerability research, appliance zero-days.

AI supply chain as target/loot/compute: stolen API keys, fraudulent resellers, prompt injection of LiteLLM.

Secondary coverage (NPR Sep 10, Reuters Sep 10) confirms: five biological research cases among disrupted uses Dec 2025-Aug 2026; suspected Russia-linked cyber espionage; Chinese labs distillation allegations (Alibaba, Moonshot, DeepSeek, Xiaomi per Sep 11 ground.news timeline – outside W37 ordinary window for distillation naming detail, report itself Sep 10 is ordinary).

Limitations: cases are notable examples, not representative picture; no claim that bioweapons were produced.
